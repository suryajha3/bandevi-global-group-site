Masala & food products

Digital systems for masala and food-product brands.

BANDEVI GLOBAL GROUP builds product websites, e-commerce stores, CRM, ERP, inventory, order workflows, distributor inquiry systems, and automation for masala and packaged food businesses.

  • ✓Premium product website with brand story, categories, product detail pages, offers, and lead paths
  • ✓E-commerce, payment, order status, inventory, invoice, and customer communication workflows
  • ✓CRM and distributor inquiry tracking for bulk orders, retail partners, repeat customers, and campaigns

What this includes.

Each page focuses on a service area that can become a standalone project or part of a larger digital system.

Product website

Brand story, product catalog, categories, nutrition or usage details, trust sections, forms, and SEO-ready pages.

Online store

Cart, checkout, payment flow, offers, order confirmation, customer notifications, and repeat purchase paths.

Order and stock control

Inventory views, order status, supplier notes, invoices, dispatch stages, and internal dashboards.

CRM and distributor leads

Capture retailer, distributor, bulk, and customer inquiries with ownership, follow-ups, and source reporting.

Business dashboards

Track product interest, order volume, campaign leads, conversion, stock movement, and customer response.

WhatsApp handoff

Route website inquiries and order questions into a clean WhatsApp or sales-team response flow.
