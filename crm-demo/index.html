CRM planning guide

Bring one real workflow to the discussion.

A useful first conversation starts with how you work today and what needs to change.

  1. Describe the bottleneck

    For example, an enquiry gets missed or a follow-up has no owner.

  2. List your users and tools

    Identify sales roles, spreadsheets, existing systems and access requirements.

  3. Choose the first release

    Prioritise the smallest workflow your team can review and use.

  4. Agree acceptance and handover

    Confirm test examples, migration checks, training and support responsibilities.

Before you request a CRM walkthrough

Can we start with one sales workflow?

Yes. Share the workflow and users you want to prioritise. The team will discuss what a useful first release includes and what should follow later.

Can you connect our existing tools?

List the tools and the data you want to exchange. Access, API availability and third-party terms must be reviewed before an integration is included in the proposal.

Can we get an international quote?

Request INR, USD, GBP or EUR on the enquiry form. The team confirms the agreed amount, payment terms and currency; choosing a currency does not convert prices automatically.

Is the sample demo a customer case study?

No. It uses fictional records to illustrate a workflow. Your project features and commercial terms are confirmed separately.

What happens after we request a demo?

The team reviews your requirements and contacts you to confirm availability and a relevant walkthrough. A preferred meeting time is a request until confirmed.
