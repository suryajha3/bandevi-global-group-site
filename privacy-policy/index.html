Privacy policy

Information we collect.

BANDEVI GLOBAL GROUP handles information to respond to inquiries, demo requests, support requests and project discussions. Optional analytics and advertising measurement use the separate choices described below.

  • ✓Contact details such as name, company, phone number, email address, and preferred contact method.
  • ✓Project details shared through contact forms, demo request forms, WhatsApp, email, phone calls, or meetings.
  • ✓Contact and demo enquiries are stored in a private inbox on our hosting server with a reference number. Sales notifications may be delivered through our email provider. A hashed network identifier is temporarily used to limit automated abuse.
  • ✓Basic website analytics and technical information such as page visits, device type, browser type, and referral source when analytics tools are enabled.
  • ✓For enquiry measurement, this browser tab may retain a source category, landing page, referring hostname and campaign labels for up to 30 minutes. Submitted enquiries include those source details in the private inbox. Referrers and campaign information may be unavailable or incomplete; organic-search attribution is inferred, not independently verified.
  • ✓Our saved-enquiry analytics event contains only the form category, a predefined service category and page/source categories. It does not include your name, email, phone, message or enquiry reference. Contact-link clicks are measured separately from saved enquiries. Analytics blockers and Do Not Track can limit these measurements. Anonymous form-journey measurement records starts, fixed error categories, saved confirmations and contact-button clicks using a random identifier held only in page memory. It does not record form contents, contact details or enquiry references, and cannot be linked to customer records. These measurements are retained for up to 90 days in the active database; temporary hashed abuse-limit identifiers expire after one hour. Do Not Track disables this measurement.

How information is used

Information is used to reply to inquiries, qualify demo requests, plan project scope, provide support, improve website content, and keep communication records clear for business follow-up.

Sharing and security

BANDEVI does not sell inquiry data. Information may be shared only with internal team members, service providers, hosting tools, analytics tools, or communication platforms when needed to support the requested work.

Your choices

You can contact BANDEVI to update contact details, ask about stored inquiry information, or request removal of non-essential communication records where legally and operationally possible.

Your analytics choice

Google Analytics and anonymous form-journey measurement stay off until you choose Allow analytics. Declining does not stop you sending an enquiry. Use Analytics preferences at the bottom of any public page to change your choice. Do Not Track keeps analytics off.

We store your analytics preference in this browser for six months. With permission, Google Analytics uses analytics cookies and receives page and event information; anonymous journey measurement records form starts, validation, failures and saved confirmations. Personal form fields are not included in these analytics events. Enquiry details and their source information are handled separately to respond to your request.

When you withdraw permission, future measurement is stopped and this site removes accessible Google Analytics cookies. Events already sent are not undone. Earlier form actions are not replayed when you later allow analytics.

Optional advertising measurement

On configured US, UK and India CRM campaign pages, a visitor arriving with a Google advertising click identifier may choose a separate, unchecked permission to share that click and later qualified-lead or won-project outcomes with Google. You can send an enquiry and book a demo without agreeing. Analytics permission alone does not enable this sharing. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control prevent this collection.

When you agree, the click identifier is kept separately from your enquiry in our protected operational database for up to 90 days, then removed by routine cleanup. Protected backup copies age out under the existing backup retention process. A reviewed Google Ads import may include the click identifier, conversion action, milestone time, stable order identifier and recorded deal value and currency. This setup does not export your name, email address, phone number or project requirements, enable ad personalisation, or automatically upload data to Google.

To withdraw, email sales@bandeviglobalgroup.com with your enquiry reference. The team can remove its stored click identifier and exclude the enquiry from future exports. Files already downloaded or data already shared with Google require separate handling; withdrawing here does not automatically retract external copies. These advertising records are separate from the information we retain to respond to your enquiry and deliver agreed work.
