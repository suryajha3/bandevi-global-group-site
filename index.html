Before work starts

Know what your proposal and handover cover.

Use this checklist in your project discussion. The agreed written proposal confirms the deliverables, responsibilities and commercial terms for your project.

1. Scope and acceptance

List pages, modules, user roles and integrations. Record exclusions, customer-supplied content and access, and the checks that demonstrate each deliverable works.

2. Milestones and changes

Agree review points, approval owners and target dates, including dependencies. Record requested changes and their effect on cost or timing before adding them to scope.

3. Price and payment triggers

Confirm the quoted amount, currency, applicable taxes and when each payment is due. Identify provider subscriptions, hosting, usage charges and recurring maintenance separately.

4. Ownership and access

Specify source-code and data rights, third-party licence limits and who holds domain, hosting and provider accounts. Agree administrator access, exports and credential transfer responsibilities.

5. Launch and handover

Agree acceptance sign-off, deployment responsibility and recovery arrangements. Name the documentation, access inventory, training and backup or export instructions included in handover.

6. Support after launch

Record the support contact, hours, response expectations and coverage period. Distinguish defect fixes from enhancements and identify renewals, maintenance charges and escalation arrangements.

What should I check before approving a milestone?

Review the agreed acceptance checks and record any unresolved items, the person responsible and the next review date. Confirm the approval and payment trigger against your written proposal.

Are source code and ongoing support automatically included?

The proposal must specify the rights, handover items and support coverage included. Third-party software may have its own licence conditions; agree these before committing.
