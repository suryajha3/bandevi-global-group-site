# Public sharing previews and enquiry confirmation — 25 October 2026

The homepage and the US, UK and India software-development and travel-agency CRM campaign pages each have a distinct branded PNG for link sharing. The seven 1200 × 630 images use the existing emerald, champagne and ivory palette, BG monogram, actual quotation currencies and remote-delivery wording. They contain no customer data, testimonials, local-office claims or results claims. `scripts/build-share-previews.cjs` contains the code-native artwork and page mapping; `--metadata` updates only those pages' image tags. Open Graph and Twitter image metadata include descriptive alternatives; PNG format, HTTPS URLs and dimensions are explicit. Platforms control their own crawl and cache timing, so previously cached shares may retain their earlier preview until refreshed.

After a valid successful enquiry response, the existing receipt now shows the submitted service, business country and quote currency, followed by its existing reference, follow-up actions and next steps. These details come from the payload sent in that request, rather than mutable fields or reset form defaults. The component uses text content and bounded service labels. Missing or unknown choices do not become inferred countries or prices. A quote and scope still require agreement; the receipt does not establish email delivery or a booked meeting. Existing server-confirmed meeting status remains authoritative.

Review remains optional. Required fields, validation, POST body, idempotency keys, retries, analytics consent, booking links, backend and private applications are unchanged. Both static client and source renderer contain the same receipt logic. Other public HTML changes refresh the client and enhancement stylesheet cache versions; page body markup, colours and layout are retained.

`scripts/qa-sharing-receipt.cjs --all-browsers` checks sharing metadata/assets, local mocked receipts, pending-request edits, reset/copy behaviour, failure and malformed-reference recovery, unchanged retry bodies/keys, safe text, home/demo forms, source rendering, gallery focus and responsive layouts in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox. Pinned axe checks cover the new receipt. A dedicated CI job runs the three engines alongside the existing full backend/browser gates.

`--live` is GET-only. It validates published metadata and images, pre-submit review keyboard behaviour, gallery navigation and detached receipt component rendering. It never submits a production enquiry or books a demo. Browser-engine emulation is not physical iPhone/Android or branded Safari certification. Connected Analytics/Search Console findings are kept in a private workspace report, not embedded in public assets or source documentation.
