## Required launch inputs

The owner must identify the Google Ads customer account and confirm a maximum daily budget for each market in that account's billing currency. Verify the account time zone, billing currency, billing setup, authorised conversion actions, geographic settings and landing-page tracking in the connected account before publishing. Keep campaigns paused until these inputs and the final campaign configuration are approved. Do not infer a spending limit from a generic “do it”.

## Measurement and sales handoff

1. Preserve each draft's exact tracked destination and test consent, submission, saved country and campaign attribution. Do not count a page view or WhatsApp click as a qualified customer.
2. Assign each real workflow-review request to a salesperson, record the next action and follow-up date, and use the CRM reviews queue to complete a written outcome.
3. Record actual sales milestones and won values in the existing enquiry workflow. Do not invent customer results or testimonials.
4. Create one manual cost profile per unique market/source/medium/campaign combination after checking the account currency and time zone. Enter actual completed daily costs, including confirmed zero days.
5. Review country and campaign enquiry counts, qualification, current Won, cost coverage and cost ratios. Compare only compatible cohorts and currencies. Partial cost coverage cannot support cost-per-customer decisions.
6. Review the account's search terms, disapprovals and delivery before proposing changes. Any budget increase requires a new specific limit. Keep a record of decisions and actual costs.

This runbook does not publish ads, send outreach, promise lead volume or claim that an advertising account is connected. Account-specific connection links belong in the private conversation, never in the repository.
