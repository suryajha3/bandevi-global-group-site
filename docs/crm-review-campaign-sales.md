# CRM review, project evidence and campaign sales

The US, UK and India software-service pages share a CRM workflow-review option in their existing regional enquiry form. It captures current tools, CRM user count, the main workflow problem and optional integrations. The CTA selects CRM without submitting or overwriting the existing message. Choosing another service turns the review off; typed review details remain until reset. A successful submission resets the form; failures retain the draft and retry key.

The optional `crmReview` object is validated server-side on the three regional contact routes, with a CRM service and structured country preferences. Required values, types, allowed keys and lengths are checked. The server appends a canonical readable summary to the persisted message, so existing sales notification and private inbox paths include the review. The complete message is capped at 3,000 characters. No automatic Qualified stage, reservation, assessment result, external integration or customer reply is created. No schema migration or second lead store is needed.

`#project-proof` links to BANDEVI's delivered internal website record, the existing blank consultation screenshot and the fictional sample CRM. Claims describe implemented functionality; no client endorsement or increased sales is inferred. Country preferences, quote currency and incoming campaign attribution remain editable through the existing enquiry flow. For browsers without JavaScript the static proof, review fields and existing contact fallback remain available; the JSON enquiry flow requires JavaScript.

The authenticated Reports view extends `/api/admin/sales-funnel?days=7|30|90` with `campaigns`: received-date cohorts grouped by saved structured business country, campaign, source, medium, channel and landing page. Countries are never inferred from phone, currency, source URL or campaign text. Untagged and legacy records remain visible under Unattributed / Country not recorded. Staff ownership and QA exclusions apply before aggregation. No customer names, email addresses, review text or reference IDs appear in aggregates.

Each group shows enquiries, CRM reviews, recorded Qualified milestones, booked versus completed demos, published proposals and current Won outcomes. Unique enquiries prevent duplicate stage events, reservations or proposal revisions from inflating milestone counts. Rates have explicit denominators. Country and campaign filters operate on the current authorised report and clear on logout; stale responses are guarded by session identity and generation.

Won deal values come exclusively from existing staff-recorded `deal_values` for currently Won enquiries. Values and counts remain separate by currency, with no FX conversion. Missing values are counted explicitly. These are recorded deal values, not cash receipts, recognised revenue, budgets or proposal totals. Ad spend is not connected: CPA, ROAS and attribution beyond the saved first-entry context are not claimed. No ad campaign, social post, message or paid subscription is launched by this change.

Validation: isolated server review validation, persistence, retries, campaign grouping, repeated milestones, currency separation, reopened deals, legacy/QA/date/role boundaries; browser checks for static/source rendering, 390px/1440px, required fields, failed-request recovery, country/currency and campaign preservation, private report filters, empty state and logout/agent isolation. Production verification is read-only.
