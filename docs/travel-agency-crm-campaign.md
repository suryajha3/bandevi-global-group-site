# Travel agency CRM campaign — 17 October 2026

The US, UK and India software-service pages each contain `#travel-agency-crm`: a country-specific offer, native video controls, downloads, transcript and an enquiry action. The action selects CRM, fills an empty travel-agency brief, and preserves existing customer text, country, currency and time-zone choices. It does not submit an enquiry or confirm an appointment.

## Walkthrough

`assets/travel-agency-crm-walkthrough.mp4` and `.webm` are silent, captioned 66-second 1280×720 videos. Screens were captured locally from the existing `/sample-crm/` with all non-GET requests blocked. The film reviews a fictional requirement, owner Ravi, a stage change and activity history, and a sample travel quotation. It ends with a project-scope discussion invitation.

Persistent labels identify fictional records and the absence of live bookings/payments. The sample INR travel values are not software prices. The walkthrough is not a customer case study or endorsement. On-screen captions, a six-cue WebVTT track and a downloadable transcript convey the content without audio. The existing 28-second introduction is retained.

Native browser recording captured the demonstration. An isolated local FFmpeg tool normalised it to 20 frames per second, H.264 MP4 and VP9 WebM, with seeking/MP4 fast start and full-decode checks. No media-generation package is added to production dependencies.

## Draft advertising pack

The promotion kit links to TXT, JSON and a CSV review worksheet. Each market has two Search variants, keyword/negative suggestions, one LinkedIn concept, sitelinks and its own tracked regional destination. Search headlines are at most 30 characters and descriptions at most 90. The source references are included in the pack.

All campaigns remain drafts. Ad budgets and ad-account currencies are unset; quotation currencies are USD, GBP and INR respectively. The CSV is a review worksheet, not an Ads Editor import. Nothing is uploaded, scheduled, boosted or launched. Account policies, geography settings, consent-aware tracking, budget and actual sales coverage require review before launch.

The earlier organic LinkedIn/Facebook publication record and captions are preserved separately. No new social message or customer outreach is sent by this release.
