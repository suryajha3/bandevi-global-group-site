# India, service offers and market reporting — 17 October 2026

- `/india/software-development/` provides India-specific planning information, INR and IST enquiry defaults, editable business-country preferences and the existing email fallback. The Markets menu links to India throughout the public corporate site.
- `/service-offers/` compares business website, sales CRM and travel portal starting scopes. Each offer names deliverables to agree, exclusions to confirm and acceptance checks. Links select the existing guided project-brief workflows. US/UK pages include the comparison; the three main service pages link to their matching offer.
- Offers are proposal starting points. They do not publish fixed prices, promised delivery dates, unsupported customer outcomes, staffed overseas offices or unapproved supplier capabilities.
- India and the existing US, UK, UAE and Singapore market pages have reciprocal regional alternates. New pages are listed in the sitemap and work in pre-rendered and source-rendered modes.

## Private country sales comparison

Team inbox → Reports → Demo-to-sale conversion → US, UK & India. Uses the existing 7/30/90-day received-date cohort selector.

Country is the validated structured `qualification.country` saved with the enquiry. US, GB, IN and explicit `other` are separate groups. Older enquiries without structured preferences remain **Country not recorded** even when a phone prefix, quote currency, message or landing page suggests a country. No historical customer records are rewritten.

Each enquiry counts once. Qualified needs a recorded Qualified event; reservations and published proposals use recorded milestones; current Won uses the current workflow stage. Demo-to-proposal and proposal-to-Won rates preserve the existing event-order checks. Current Won / enquiry is also shown separately from the demo route. Empty denominators have no percentage. The table aggregates counts and rates only, without customer fields or combined-currency revenue.

Deployment QA is excluded. Agents see assigned enquiries only; managers and administrators retain their existing access. Sign-out clears the country table and stale session/date-range responses cannot populate a later report.
