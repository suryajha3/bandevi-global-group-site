Assess the evidence

Make the reference useful for your decision.

Confirm the delivery role

Ask which parts BANDEVI designed, built or maintained, what depended on third-party providers, and what the client accepted.

Check the outcome source

Ask how a result was measured, over which dates and against which starting point. A target or software mockup does not establish a delivered result.

Respect client permission

Request only material approved for sharing. Screens and exports should hide private customer records, credentials and commercially sensitive information.
