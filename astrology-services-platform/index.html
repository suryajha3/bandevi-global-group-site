Booking requirements

Agree the consultation journey before building.

Use this example to scope software for an astrology business. BANDEVI provides development services; consultation advice and service claims belong to the consultation provider.

Choose and request

Show consultation format, duration, provider, price and cancellation terms. Define time zones and whether a slot is a request or an immediately confirmed booking.

Confirm and deliver

Agree payment-provider access, confirmation rules, reminders and rescheduling. Decide how the team delivers a call, meeting link or report and records its completion.

Handle exceptions

Specify failed payments, unavailable slots, duplicate requests, no-shows and refund reviews. Give each exception an owner and an auditable status.

Privacy and access

Collect only the information needed for the service.

Birth details, contact information and consultation notes need a defined purpose, customer notice, access rules and retention policy. Discuss these requirements before collecting real client records.

Confirm who can view or download reports, how links expire, how customers request corrections or deletion, and how backups and exports are handled. Agree the controls and test them using sample data.

Bring to the project discussion

  • ✓Consultation types, appointment rules and staff roles
  • ✓A sample booking and report using fictitious details
  • ✓Payment, calendar and communication provider requirements
  • ✓Cancellation, refund and privacy notices
  • ✓Acceptance checks, training and support expectations

Discuss your platform requirements

Questions about astrology website development.

Does the platform automatically produce astrology calculations?

Booking and customer management do not include a calculation engine by default. Specify any calculation, chart or report integration separately, including provider rights, data requirements, accuracy checks and costs.

Can we start with consultation enquiries?

Yes. Scope service pages and enquiry capture first, then agree payment, slot confirmation, CRM and portal modules when the workflow is clear.

What determines the project price and timeline?

Modules, user roles, design, migration and external integrations determine scope. Request a written proposal with deliverables, provider fees, ownership, milestones and support terms.
