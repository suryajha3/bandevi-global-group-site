B2C travel portal · Project reference

Trip Sarathi: a clear starting point for travel discovery.

A public homepage with travel-service navigation and a flight-search entry point. BANDEVI GLOBAL GROUP identifies Trip Sarathi as a B2C travel portal development project.

Development attribution is supplied by BANDEVI’s owner. The observations below come from the public interface captured on 6 October 2026; they do not establish the delivery date or complete contracted scope.

The public customer journey

Help a traveller choose a service and begin a search.

The reviewed homepage presents travel-service navigation alongside a flight-search form. These visible entry points give a visitor a place to begin exploring travel options.

1. Choose a travel service

Homepage navigation introduces travel-service entry points. A visitor can start by choosing the service relevant to their trip.

2. Begin a flight search

The dated public screenshot shows a flight-search interface. This is evidence of a visible search entry point, rather than a verified account of supplier responses or completed bookings.

3. Define the next step

For a similar project, agree how a visitor moves from search into results, an enquiry or booking. Those later steps are requirements to discuss for your project; their delivery is not established by this screenshot.
