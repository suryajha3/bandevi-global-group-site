Internal implementation · reviewed 12 October 2026

Walk through the UK and US enquiry journey

These screenshots show BANDEVI’s own public website. They document implemented interfaces, not customer results.

  1. Choose a first-release scope. Buyers review websites and apps, CRM and ERP, travel portals or automation. Examples guide the discussion; the written proposal defines included work.
  2. Provide useful planning context. Country, budget and target launch window are optional. GBP or USD quote preferences can be changed. No fixed price or delivery promise is inferred from a selection.
  3. Save an enquiry and retain its context. The existing enquiry service receives the planning details in the message and keeps the source page. A failed request retains the draft; the successful response supplies a reference.
  4. Review the lead separately. The team confirms the business problem, decision maker, country, budget and next step. A submitted form is not automatically a qualified opportunity.

One existing enquiry service

The regional forms reuse the existing submission contract. Planning details are included in the enquiry message, so the public update did not require a new backend or a parallel lead store.

Minimal analytics fields

A successful saved enquiry adds an allowlisted market code: GB, US or other. Names, email addresses, budget and message text are excluded from the event. A default country selection needs confirmation during follow-up.

Progressive access

The scope guidance is published in HTML. Visitors without JavaScript can read the evidence and use the email fallback. Desktop and mobile checks cover both layouts.

Verification record

The 12 October release passed 10 regional enquiry checks, 25 existing enquiry journey checks and eight content, link and responsive checks. Submission responses were simulated locally; these tests did not create production leads. Public release files matched the deployed files.

Both regional URLs were confirmed indexed, and Google accepted recrawl requests on 12 October. Recrawl acceptance does not prove that Google has processed the revised content. Search ranking gains, increased enquiries and customer revenue remain unmeasured.

Static page content, working internal links, keyboard controls and desktop/mobile layouts were checked. Required fields and the brief review step were tested before a simulated submission.

Private sales review

The implementation supports enquiry stages, owners, follow-up dates and structured brief review in an authenticated inbox. This record does not expose production enquiry records or customer information.

Public deployment review

The public pages and screenshots were reviewed after deployment. The service health check confirms that the enquiry service responds; it does not establish customer conversion or email delivery for every request.

Outcome boundary: these checks establish implemented behaviour. No conversion increase, customer revenue result or client testimonial is claimed.

What was checked

Public pages were checked for readable content without JavaScript, unique titles, valid structured data and working internal links.
