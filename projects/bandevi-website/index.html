BANDEVI website: from service discovery to a focused enquiry

An internal website improvement project for bandeviglobalgroup.com. Explore the actual public pages and the work completed to make services and next steps clearer.

Project type: BANDEVI’s own website. This is not a third-party client testimonial or evidence of customer revenue results.

Explore the live website

The problem addressed

The homepage needed to explain three service areas clearly and guide visitors into a relevant conversation. Earlier layouts pushed the selected demo action lower on the screen and used travel imagery for every service choice.

The scope completed

  • Responsive homepage with three selectable service previews.
  • Matching website, CRM and travel workflow concept visuals.
  • Visible demo action and links to detailed service pages.
  • Service and walkthrough preselection on the demo form.
  • Enquiry forms with confirmation and retained drafts when submission fails.
  • Public company-record and project-evidence routes.
  • Three-step project brief with editable requirements, budget preference and a review step.
  • Travel website, CRM and ERP comparison with tailored brief starting points.
  • Sample CRM walkthrough using fictional records.
  • Proposal and handover review checklist.
  • US and UK software-service pages describing remote delivery from Gurugram.
  • Saved-enquiry measurement and separate categories for contact-link clicks.

Internal project update · 11 October 2026

A clearer enquiry journey for US and UK buyers

The next phase made remote service coverage explicit and connected the regional pages to BANDEVI’s existing project-planning journey. The delivery team is presented as based in Gurugram, India.

The buyer’s problem

An overseas buyer needs to understand who will deliver the software, which services are available and what must be agreed before work starts. A location label alone does not answer those questions.

The delivered change

Separate US and UK pages cover website and app development, CRM and ERP, travel technology, e-commerce, customer portals and business automation. They explain remote reviews, scope, dependencies, acceptance and handover.

The enquiry handover

Visitors can prepare an editable project brief before sending their contact details. Saved-enquiry measurement retains the entry page, while phone, WhatsApp and email link clicks use separate contact-method categories.

Validation and outcome

Desktop and mobile checks covered readable page content, working links, layout and regional metadata. Publication checks confirmed that the regional pages, service-location page and sitemap were served correctly. Google accepted indexing requests for both regional pages on 11 October 2026; that confirms submission to its crawl queue, rather than completed indexing.

Analytics was checked for saved-enquiry and contact-click collection. Successful enquiry submissions are configured as key events. Contact-method reporting was registered on 11 October 2026 for future processed data; a link click does not prove that a call, conversation or sale occurred.

Results reported: implemented pages and workflows, plus verified measurement configuration. US/UK enquiry growth, conversion uplift, search-ranking gains and customer revenue outcomes have not yet been established.

Functionality and evidence

What the enquiry checks establish

Local checks used simulated saved-enquiry responses and isolated test data. They checked review-before-send, saved-reference feedback, retained drafts after a failed request, editable travel brief suggestions and structured requirements in the private sales workflow.

Customer-facing checks

Static page content, working internal links, keyboard controls and desktop/mobile layouts were checked. Required fields and the brief review step were tested before a simulated submission.

Private sales review

The implementation supports enquiry stages, owners, follow-up dates and structured brief review in an authenticated inbox. This record does not expose production enquiry records or customer information.

Public deployment review

The public pages and screenshots were reviewed after deployment. The service health check confirms that the enquiry service responds; it does not establish customer conversion or email delivery for every request.

Outcome boundary: these checks establish implemented behaviour. No conversion increase, customer revenue result or client testimonial is claimed.

What was checked

Desktop and mobile layouts, service-image switching, keyboard tabs, visible demo buttons, stable preview heights and service preselection were checked. Submission success and failure paths were tested with simulated responses; no live customer enquiry was used for those checks.

Public pages were checked for readable content without JavaScript, unique titles, valid structured data and working internal links.

How to interpret this project

This page demonstrates completed website functionality. Conversion uplift, traffic growth and customer revenue outcomes have not been established for this showcase.

Client case studies, testimonials and business registrations require their own supporting records. They are not implied by this internal project.
