How to review a supplied record

  • Match the legal name to the business that contracts and invoices your project.
  • Check the issuer, date, scope and any official verification route.
  • Separate company-authored notes from records issued by an authority, auditor or other qualified source.
  • Ask which documents may be shared publicly and which should be reviewed privately.

Website statements, social posts and workplace photos alone do not verify financial figures, registrations or individual offices.
