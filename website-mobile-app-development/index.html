Know what you will review and receive.

Content, scope and owners

Access, documentation and support

Confirm what your team receives and who owns the ongoing responsibilities.

  • Hosting, domain and editing access

  • Support coverage, hours and escalation contact
Confirm integrations and ongoing costs. CRM, ERP, payment providers and app-store releases need the relevant accounts, approvals and supported interfaces. Your proposal identifies third-party fees and the responsibilities of each party.

Questions before you start.

Can we start with a website and add a portal later?

Yes, the first release can focus on service pages and enquiry capture. Agree later account features, shared records and integrations separately.

Who provides content and existing accounts?

Your proposal names the copy, image and approval owners, migration requirements and the accounts needed. Confirm any content creation included in our scope.

Are hosting, app-store approval and ongoing support included?

Only as agreed in the proposal. Confirm hosting and provider fees, store account ownership, release responsibilities and support coverage. Store review decisions are controlled by the platform.
