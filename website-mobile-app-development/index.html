Questions before you start.

Can we start with a website and add a portal later?

Yes, the first release can focus on service pages and enquiry capture. Agree later account features, shared records and integrations separately.

Who provides content and existing accounts?

Your proposal names the copy, image and approval owners, migration requirements and the accounts needed. Confirm any content creation included in our scope.

Are hosting, app-store approval and ongoing support included?

Only as agreed in the proposal. Confirm hosting and provider fees, store account ownership, release responsibilities and support coverage. Store review decisions are controlled by the platform.

Questions to settle before your proposal.

What should we share for a website estimate?

Share your current URL, audience, service list, expected pages and two or three actions visitors need to complete. Tell us whether copy, images, domain and hosting are available. The estimate follows a review of that scope.

Can you improve our existing website?

We can review its navigation, mobile layout, forms and content before recommending changes. For a rebuild, agree the content migration and old-URL redirects so important customer journeys remain accessible.

How do you plan enquiry tracking?

Agree which form submissions, phone links and WhatsApp clicks matter. Tracking a link click does not prove that a call or conversation happened; confirmed enquiries should be measured separately.

When can a website or app launch?

Timing depends on page or screen count, approved content, integrations and review availability. Your written proposal sets milestones. Mobile-store review and third-party account approvals have their own timelines.
