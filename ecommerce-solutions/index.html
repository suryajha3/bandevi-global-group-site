From products to fulfilment

Define the rules behind each order.

A storefront needs reliable content and clear operational responsibilities. Your written proposal confirms what is included, who supplies it and how it will be tested.

Products and discovery

Agree categories, product identifiers, variants, images, approved descriptions, prices and availability. Plan mobile navigation and product-page content that helps customers compare options.

Checkout and payments

Define delivery areas, shipping charges, applicable tax rules and payment-provider requirements with your business advisers. Test failed payments, retries and duplicate confirmations; an order status alone is not proof of settlement.

Orders and customer support

Agree who reviews new orders, updates dispatch status and handles cancellation, return and refund requests. Your team supplies and approves the customer-facing policies.

Launch and handover

Review mobile product selection, checkout and confirmation using test orders. Confirm admin access, product editing, backups, training, provider charges and post-launch support.

Plan before you build

Questions to settle before your proposal.

Can we launch a catalogue before adding checkout?

Yes. A first release can show products and collect enquiries. Cart, payments and order processing can follow as separately agreed scope. Plan product identifiers and future data requirements early.

Can the store connect to CRM or inventory software?

Connections depend on the available APIs, permissions and record definitions. Confirm which system owns stock and customer data, how updates are synchronised and who handles failed or delayed updates.

What do you need to estimate the project?

Share product and variant counts, a sample catalogue, selling regions, delivery rules, preferred payment provider and current software. Add your target launch window and the content your team can supply.

Are hosting, gateway charges and maintenance included?

Only where the proposal says so. Confirm domain and hosting ownership, payment-provider charges, third-party subscriptions, support coverage and ongoing product-content responsibilities before starting.
