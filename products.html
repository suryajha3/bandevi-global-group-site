How to choose the right package.

The best first package is the one that fixes the most urgent sales, operations, trust, customer-service, or reporting problem.

Need online trust?

Start with a website, travel website, white-label website, or product-commerce package.

Need lead control?

Start with CRM or a business website + CRM package so every enquiry has an owner and next action.

Need operations control?

Start with CRM + ERP when orders, bookings, suppliers, documents, and payments need structure.

Need customer service control?

Start with a portal or automation package to reduce repeated manual communication.

Can BANDEVI support multi-office and global teams?
