Questions before you start.

How do we start, and what happens after the enquiry?

Choose a service and describe one customer or staff workflow. The team reviews your request and contacts you to clarify scope and arrange a conversation. A saved enquiry reference confirms receipt; it does not confirm a meeting or a project commitment.

Can we request a GBP quotation?

Yes. BANDEVI supports USD, GBP and INR quotations and invoicing. The form starts with GBP as your preference. Your written proposal confirms the currency, amount, payment milestones, applicable taxes and separately listed third-party costs.

How long will the project take?

Timing depends on the agreed scope, integrations, content, access and review decisions. Share your target launch window; the team assesses feasibility before proposing milestones. A date entered in the form is a planning request, not a confirmed delivery date.

Who owns the source code, data and accounts?

Agree source-code and data rights, third-party licences, domain and hosting ownership, exports and administrator access in the written proposal. Identify the documentation, training and account-transfer steps included in handover before work begins.

What support is included after launch?

Confirm the support period, hours, contact, response expectations and maintenance charges in the proposal. Separate defect fixes from new features, and identify who manages hosting, provider subscriptions and renewals.

How will our UK team work with BANDEVI remotely?

The delivery team is in Gurugram, India. Office and consultation hours are Monday–Saturday, 10:30 am–7:30 pm IST (UTC+05:30); Sunday closed. Include GMT or BST when arranging reviews. Specific meeting times and support coverage are agreed with the team; this page describes remote service coverage.
