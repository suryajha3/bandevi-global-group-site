Questions before you start.

Do you have a customer-facing UK office?

This page describes remote service coverage from India. BANDEVI does not present a staffed UK office here. Arrange project discussions through the published business contact channels.

How should a UK team prepare for a remote software project?

Bring one representative customer or staff workflow, a list of current systems, required reports and the people responsible for review. Include date formats, GMT/BST scheduling, currency display and any procurement or data-handling requirements to assess.

Are integrations and ongoing support included?

Only when specified in the proposal. Share your current systems and available provider documentation. Confirm access, fees, support hours and responsibilities before adding an integration or maintenance plan.

Bring one workflow to the first discussion.

Tell us your service interest, current process, desired first release, country and time zone. Use fictional or anonymised examples initially; include your preferred quotation currency and launch window.

What affects custom software development cost?

The number of workflows and user roles, integrations, data migration, reporting and acceptance tests determine the development scope. Hosting, third-party licenses and ongoing support can add recurring costs. Ask for these to be separated in the proposal.

Bring one representative workflow to discovery. Agree the smallest useful first release, its acceptance criteria and what will be deferred before comparing quotes. A budget range on the form is a planning input; it does not set a fixed price.
