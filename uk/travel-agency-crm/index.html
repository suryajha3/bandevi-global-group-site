Questions before you start

Is this a fixed-price CRM subscription?

No fixed package price is published here. We discuss the workflow, users, integrations and support, then provide an agreed written scope and quotation in GBP or another agreed currency.

Can we review times in our local zone?

Yes. The booking step shows published slots in your selected time zone, with daylight-saving changes applied and India reference times. Meeting availability and joining details require confirmation.

What should we bring to a review?

Bring your sales stages, an anonymised enquiry and the main handover problem. Do not send customer records, supplier credentials or passwords.
