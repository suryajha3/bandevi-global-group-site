How we work

A delivery process with clear responsibilities.

Your written proposal confirms deliverables, review points, costs, dependencies and handover items.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Map the business problem, users and current workflow with you.

  2. 02

    Agree the scope

    Confirm modules, exclusions, acceptance checks and named review owners.

  3. 03

    Build & review

    Review the screens and test the agreed customer and staff journeys.

  4. 04

    Launch & hand over

    Agree access, documentation, training, recovery and ongoing support responsibilities.
