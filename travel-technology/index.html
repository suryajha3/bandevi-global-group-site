From scope to handover

Know what your proposal includes.

Each build has an agreed scope. Review the deliverables, dependencies and acceptance checks before development begins.

01 · Define

Pages, records and responsibilities

Agree customer journeys, staff roles, modules, required fields and reports. Name the content and review owners.

  • Included features and exclusions
  • Review stages and acceptance checks
  • Milestones and payment terms
02 · Connect & review

Test the agreed travel journeys

Review enquiry capture, quote changes, booking handover and exceptions that apply to your scope.

  • Supplier and payment-provider access
  • Approvals, third-party charges and data mapping
  • Failed requests, retries and role restrictions
03 · Hand over

Agree access and support

Confirm the items your team receives and the responsibilities after launch.

  • Account access, source-code and data rights
  • Documentation, training and backup arrangements
  • Support coverage, hours and escalation contact
Live booking depends on approved integrations. Flight, hotel and payment connections are included only when named in the proposal and provider access is approved. Pricing and dates follow requirements review.
