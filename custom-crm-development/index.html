Custom CRM Development · remote UK and US coverage

Custom CRM development for the way your team sells.

Connect enquiries, customer records, quotations and follow-ups around a defined sales process. BANDEVI develops custom CRM software from Gurugram, India, with remote service coverage for UK and US businesses.

Project fit · Scope · Delivery · Cost planning · Questions

When this service is a useful fit

Consider a bespoke CRM when your sales stages, permissions or handover rules require repeated workarounds in existing tools. Start with one team and one measurable workflow before expanding the system.

If an established CRM can handle your process through configuration, compare that option before commissioning a custom build. Include subscription, setup, migration, administration and future change costs in the decision.

Define the first-release scope

Use the areas below to agree what the first release must do. Features, access and external connections are confirmed in the project proposal.

Lead and customer records

Define contacts, companies, enquiry sources, record ownership and duplicate-review rules. Agree which data is needed and who may view or edit it.

Pipeline and quotations

Specify stages, exit criteria, quotation versions and next actions. Decide what must be complete before an opportunity moves to delivery.

Follow-up and reporting

Plan tasks, due dates, activity history and reports around decisions the team makes. Separate received enquiries, qualified discussions and agreed work.

Roles and handover

Map sales, management and support permissions. Test reassignment, restricted records and the information passed into operations or ERP.

A delivery plan you can review

Agree the outputs and review owner for each stage before work begins.

  1. 1. Map one sales process

    Bring an anonymised enquiry and its journey to quotation. Identify repeated entry, missed follow-ups and the person who owns each decision.

  2. 2. Agree the first-release specification

    Review fields, screens, permissions, reports and integration dependencies. Record exclusions, acceptance examples and a change-approval process.

  3. 3. Build and review the agreed workflow

    Use representative test records to review assignment, stage changes, quotations and reporting. Include restricted-access and failed-integration cases.

  4. 4. Prepare migration and handover

    Agree trial import results, training, deployment checks, rollback, documentation and support responsibilities before the production cutover.

Integrations and migration

For each website, email, accounting or messaging connection, identify the system of record, permitted API access, consent requirements, sync direction, duplicate handling and failure owner. Provider approval, subscriptions and usage charges must be assessed before a connection is included in scope.

Before moving contacts or opportunities, map fields, owners, duplicates, communication preferences and attachments. Agree a sample import and reconciliation checks, then define the cutover and rollback. Migration effort depends on source-data quality and access.

What affects cost and delivery time?

The estimate depends on the number of workflows and roles, quotation rules, reporting, data cleanup, integration complexity and launch support. Ask for first-release development, migration, hosting, third-party subscriptions and ongoing support to be itemised separately.

Request a written quotation in GBP, USD or INR. Agree assumptions, exclusions, milestone payments, acceptance and changes before work starts. A planning budget or target date is not a confirmed offer.

Agree ownership and support before launch

Confirm source-code and data rights, third-party licences, hosting access, exports, documentation, training, backup and rollback responsibilities in the contract. Define support hours, escalation routes and the treatment of future changes.

Inspect relevant work before deciding

Use fictional CRM records to discuss screens and workflow behaviour. The public sample is not a customer case study or evidence that every proposed module or integration is included.

Questions to resolve before commissioning

Use these answers to prepare a focused project discussion.

When is custom CRM development a good fit?

When a defined business process needs records, permissions or handover rules that your existing tools cannot support economically. Compare configuration and integration options before replacing a working system.

Can we keep our existing tools?

That can be considered during discovery. The proposal should identify which system owns each record and which connections are feasible with the access and provider terms available.

How much will a bespoke CRM cost?

BANDEVI provides a written quotation after reviewing scope and dependencies. GBP, USD and INR are available quotation currencies; no fixed price or launch date is implied by this page.

Who owns the code and customer data?

Agree source-code rights, data rights, repository access, third-party licences, hosting accounts and export formats in the contract before development starts.

What should we bring to the first discussion?

Your sales stages, expected users, current tools, an anonymised record, required reports, budget range if known and target launch window. Avoid sharing live customer data in an initial enquiry.
