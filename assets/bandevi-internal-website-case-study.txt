BANDEVI WEBSITE DEVELOPMENT — INTERNAL PROJECT CASE STUDY
Reviewed 11 October 2026

Project: BANDEVI’s own website, bandeviglobalgroup.com.
This is an internal project, not a customer testimonial.

THE PURPOSE
Help visitors understand the available software services, inspect relevant
workflows and prepare a useful enquiry. For US and UK buyers, make remote
delivery from Gurugram, India explicit and explain what to agree before work.

DELIVERED WORK
- Service previews with focused demo journeys.
- An editable project brief with requirements, budget/timing and review.
- Travel website, CRM and ERP comparison and a fictional sample CRM.
- US and UK pages covering all current software services.
- Accurate US/UK remote-service coverage on the locations page.
- Saved-enquiry measurement with entry-page attribution.
- Fixed categories for phone, WhatsApp and email link clicks.

VALIDATION
Desktop/mobile layouts, readable content, internal links and regional SEO
metadata were checked. The deployed regional files matched the release.
Google accepted both regional-page indexing requests on 11 October 2026.
Saved-enquiry collection and key-event configuration were checked in
Analytics. Contact-method reporting was registered on 11 October 2026
for future processed reporting.

WHAT THESE RESULTS MEAN
The evidence establishes implemented pages, workflows and measurement
configuration. It does not establish completed Google indexing, ranking
gains, US/UK enquiry growth, conversion uplift or customer revenue.
A contact-link click is not proof of a completed call, enquiry or sale.
Screenshots in the project record show public interfaces; no customer
records are exposed. The sample CRM uses fictional records.
