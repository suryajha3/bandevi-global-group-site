BANDEVI INTERNAL ERP HANDOVER WALKTHROUGH
Implementation and acceptance notes - 15 October 2026
Public demonstration: https://bandeviglobalgroup.com/travel-erp/#erp-handover-walkthrough

Purpose
An internally built browser prototype for reviewing supplier confirmations,
financial exceptions and handover requirements. All records and amounts are
fictional. It is not a customer case study, a deployed ERP, a booking service,
bank reconciliation evidence or a claim about included commercial features.

Implemented scope
- Four views: accepted quotation, supplier checks, finance review, handover.
- Two supplier confirmation checks plus finance and document review checks.
- A visible list of unresolved checks and a handover-review readiness summary.
- Fictional USD/GBP scenario labels. Amounts are relabelled, not converted.
- A regional Travel ERP enquiry link with the service preselected.
- Reset; semantic keyboard-operable controls; readable content without JavaScript.

Fictional records
Customer agreed amount: 6,000
Recorded received amount: 1,800
Customer balance: 4,200
Supplier obligation: 4,000
Recorded supplier payment: 1,000
Supplier balance: 3,000
The customer and supplier balances are separate. These amounts do not establish
bank settlement, cancellation rules, taxes, margin or accounting treatment.

Acceptance scenarios
1. Initial state has four unresolved checks.
2. Confirming accommodation alone leaves transfer, finance and documents open.
3. Completing all checks changes the summary to ready for internal handover review.
4. Reopening any check restores the unresolved state and names the open item.
5. Selecting UK uses GBP labels and opens the UK enquiry with Travel ERP selected.
6. Selecting US uses USD labels and opens the US enquiry with Travel ERP selected.
7. Reset returns the entry market and clears all four checks. Explicit regional
   links select GB or US; an absent or unrecognised market defaults to US.
8. Keyboard users can operate steps, checks, market selection and reset.
9. Without JavaScript all four sample views and direct market links remain readable.
10. No demo control submits an enquiry, booking, supplier request or payment.

Implementation boundary
Files: assets/erp-handover-demo.js and assets/erp-handover-demo.css.
Controls use fixed page data and in-memory state. Reload resets the checks.
No database, account authentication, supplier integration, accounting connection,
live inventory, payment collection or production booking was implemented here.

Before commissioning a production system
Agree record identifiers, roles and permissions, evidence behind each status,
provider access, retry and duplicate rules, migration, exports, audit records,
acceptance owners, hosting, source/data rights, training and support in writing.
The demonstration files and these notes are not a substitute for that agreement.
