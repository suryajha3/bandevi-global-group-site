AreaDiscuss in scopeExample check

Integrations and data

Confirm the dependencies before committing.

Ask which integrations are live in the demonstration, which need development and which depend on third-party access. Confirm software licensing, provider fees, migration limits and support responsibilities in the written proposal.

Migration and rollout checklist

Buyer questions

What to clarify before you start.

Is the dashboard image a delivered customer system?

No. Visuals and workflow examples on these pages illustrate concepts. Request a relevant demonstration and ask which screens, modules and integrations are available for your scope.

Should we compare CRM software with custom development?

Compare workflow fit, ownership, licensing, extensibility, migration and ongoing support. Read the Travel CRM development page and the white-label versus custom CRM guide before choosing.

Can we see a client case study?

Ask for an approved client reference relevant to your requirements. The public project models are illustrative and are not verified customer results.

What should we bring to a demo?

Bring an anonymized enquiry, your current stages, user roles, quotation example and the reports you need. Customer personal information is not needed for the first conversation.
