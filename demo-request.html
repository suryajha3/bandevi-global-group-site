Your walkthrough agenda

Know what we will discuss before you request a demo.

Use this agenda to plan a focused conversation. The team confirms which demonstration screens are available and agrees the meeting format with you.

1. Understand your workflow

Describe one task you want to improve, the people involved and the tools you use today. Choose the outcome you want to review first.

2. Walk through a relevant example

Review the available example from enquiry to next action, including the records, roles and reports relevant to your request. Identify what needs configuration or development.

3. Agree a practical next step

List open questions, required integrations and the proposed first release. Decide whether to prepare a written scope and quote, arrange another discussion or pause.

Bring these three things

  • Your main business problem and current tools.
  • An anonymized example of a task, enquiry or report.
  • The people who will use or approve the proposed system.

Please leave out passwords, payment details and customer personal information.

Leave with clearer requirements

Use the discussion to distinguish available demonstration features from proposed development and third-party dependencies. Any price, delivery date, ownership or support commitment belongs in the written proposal.

What happens after you send?

1. Keep your enquiry reference

A successful submission shows your saved enquiry reference on screen. Keep it for follow-up; submitting a request does not reserve a meeting.

2. Confirm the meeting details

The team reviews your selected service and priorities, then contacts you to agree availability, the format and the relevant demonstration. Your preferred time is a request until confirmed.

3. Review the proposed scope

After the discussion, agree the next action. If you proceed, review the written scope, dependencies, pricing, acceptance checks and support arrangements before approving work.
