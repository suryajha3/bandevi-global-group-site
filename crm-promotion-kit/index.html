A seven-day CRM promotion plan

Use the existing captions and their source-specific links. This is a suggested plan, not a scheduled or published campaign.

  1. Day 1 · Introduce the workflow. Review the LinkedIn draft and pair it with the downloadable CRM card. Keep its tracked link in the post.
  2. Day 2 · Show the example. Use the Facebook draft with the fictional walkthrough. Explain the difference between the demo and the internal website project.
  3. Day 3 · Share a visual. Use the Instagram draft with the CRM card. Put the tracked destination in an available profile or story link; caption URLs may not be clickable.
  4. Day 4 · Invite a discussion. Prepare the WhatsApp and email drafts for the audience you have selected. Keep each channel’s own tracked link.
  5. Days 5–6 · Handle responses. In Team inbox, assign each new enquiry an owner, record the business problem, and set a next action with its due date.
  6. Day 7 · Review the evidence. In Reports, select the last seven days and find crm_workflow_launch_202610. Review saved enquiries and their current stages before deciding what to repeat.

Before launch

  1. Confirm the advertising account and its currency; choose a budget per market.
  2. Review destinations, keyword suggestions, negative keywords and location presence settings. No search-volume or ROI claim is supplied.
  3. Check the account-specific ad policies, assets and tracking setup before upload.
  4. Use consent-aware attribution and validate saved enquiry, qualified lead and Won definitions before bidding on conversions.
  5. Publish only the meeting times the team can reliably cover; confirm response and support windows.
  6. Review real lead quality, demo attendance and proposals by campaign and business country before increasing spend.
