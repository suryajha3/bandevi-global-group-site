Questions before you start.

Can we begin with one team or workflow?

Yes. Agree a first release around one complete sales or operational journey, then review later modules separately.

Can existing records be migrated?

Review an anonymised sample first. Agree field mapping, duplicates, missing values, migration checks and who approves the imported records.

Are integrations and support included automatically?

The proposal names the integrations, provider dependencies, third-party charges, training and support coverage included. Availability and delivery dates follow requirements review.
