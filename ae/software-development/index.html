Reviews and regional requirements

Use Asia/Dubai when proposing a review time. Identify the customer approver, branches and any English or Arabic content requirements. Translation, right-to-left layouts and regional integrations are separately scoped and reviewed.

Currency and commercial terms

BANDEVI supports project quotations and invoicing in USD, GBP and INR, as confirmed by the owner. The written proposal specifies the contracting entity, chosen currency, amount, payment milestones, applicable taxes and third-party charges.

Data and account responsibilities

Identify the data categories, account owners and permissions in scope. Discuss procurement, security, retention and hosting requirements before granting access. Commitments must be reviewed and recorded in the project agreement.

Acceptance and handover

Agree a representative customer or staff task as an acceptance check. Confirm source-code rights, domain and hosting ownership, exports, documentation, training and support.

Questions before starting.

Can you plan software for a Dubai or UAE business remotely?

Yes. This page describes remote delivery from India. Share the business workflow, required languages, user roles and systems you need to connect. Confirm review times and acceptance criteria in the proposal.

Are Arabic screens and UAE integrations included?

They are included only when agreed in scope. Provide approved translations, right-to-left requirements and provider documentation for review. Availability, provider approval and fees must be confirmed before an integration is promised.
