About these counts

Follow-up dates use India time (Asia/Kolkata). Stage counts include closed enquiries. Follow-up and attention queues exclude Won, Lost and deployment QA records. Counts respect staff access and are independent of the current inbox filters. Needs attention includes unassigned, overdue, missing dates or next actions, and enquiries still New after 48 hours. Daily follow-up reminders use active assigned accounts, with administrator fallback. After 48 hours without recorded first contact, a daily email alert goes to the active owner or administrator. Connected calls, sent emails and recorded Contacted, Qualified or Proposal stage events count as first contact. Closed and QA enquiries are excluded. Queued alerts are rechecked before delivery; delivery depends on email configuration.
