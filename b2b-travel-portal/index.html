When this service is a useful fit

Consider a B2B portal when agencies, branches or corporate desks need controlled access to products, booking requests, documents and account-specific workflows. Define who owns each booking and commercial decision before adding suppliers.

If the requirement is a customer-facing site or a branded catalogue with enquiry forms, assess that smaller scope first. A partner booking portal introduces account permissions, supplier dependencies and financial responsibilities beyond a public website.

Define the first-release scope

Use the areas below to agree what the first release must do. Features, access and external connections are confirmed in the project proposal.

Partner accounts and permissions

Define agency ownership, account approval and user roles. Specify which records each partner can access and who can approve account changes.

Products and booking workflow

Choose the initial products and the steps from search or request to confirmation, amendment and cancellation. Keep supplier acceptance distinct from an entered status.

Commercial and finance rules

Agree who controls pricing, credit or wallet rules if required, invoices and reconciliation. Keep partner commercial information separate from customer-facing documents.

Operations and reporting

Plan queues, document access, exception ownership and reports. Retain the correct agency reference across the workflow and define the handover to CRM or ERP.

A delivery plan you can review

Agree the outputs and review owner for each stage before work begins.

  1. 1. Map the distribution model

    Identify agencies, branches, products and responsible teams. Use a fictional booking to map the partner journey and its approval points.

  2. 2. Review supplier access and dependencies

    Confirm supplier contracts, API documentation, sandbox access and relevant commercial permissions. Separate proposed connections from approved access.

  3. 3. Agree the first booking journey

    Define account boundaries, record ownership, payment responsibilities and acceptance tests for successful, failed and duplicate requests.

  4. 4. Prepare testing and controlled launch

    Agree supplier acceptance checks, user training, deployment, rollback, reconciliation and support. Release timing depends on the agreed scope and external approvals.

Integrations and migration

Flight, hotel and other supplier connections require appropriate contracts, approved access, technical documentation and testing. Supplier availability, onboarding, usage charges and payment-provider requirements are dependencies, not automatically included inventory. A public demonstration does not establish a live supplier connection.

For an existing agent network, review account ownership, agency identifiers, balances if applicable, bookings and document access. Agree which records can be migrated, how they will be reconciled and who approves the cutover.

What affects cost and delivery time?

The estimate depends on product scope, partner roles, supplier connections, commercial rules, migration and operational reporting. Request separate figures for development, supplier onboarding, API or transaction charges, hosting and support. Integration feasibility must be reviewed before price and dates are confirmed.

Request a written quotation in GBP, USD or INR. Agree assumptions, exclusions, milestone payments, acceptance and changes before work starts. A planning budget or target date is not a confirmed offer.

Agree ownership and support before launch

Confirm source-code and data rights, third-party licences, hosting access, exports, documentation, training, backup and rollback responsibilities in the contract. Define support hours, escalation routes and the treatment of future changes.

Questions to resolve before commissioning

Use these answers to prepare a focused project discussion.

What is the difference between a B2B and a B2C travel portal?

A B2B portal serves authenticated agencies or partners with defined ownership and commercial permissions. A B2C site serves travellers directly. Specify the navigation, access and payment responsibilities of each experience separately.

Are flight and hotel APIs included?

No specific supplier connection or inventory is promised by this page. The proposal must identify approved suppliers, access requirements, products, charges and testing responsibilities.

Can we start with one travel product or partner group?

A limited first release can be assessed around one product set and defined users. The scope should include account permissions, booking exceptions, documents and reconciliation before wider rollout.

Can BANDEVI quote for a UK or US project?

Yes. Remote discovery and delivery are available from India, with GBP, USD or INR quotation preferences. Supplier and provider charges need to be distinguished from development costs.

What should we prepare before requesting a proposal?

Describe your agents, travel products, current tools, supplier access, booking ownership and financial approval rules. Add expected users, target timing and a budget range if available; do not send credentials in the enquiry.
